# Laxmi Driving Launch

Build a complete professional modern responsive website for a Car and Bike Driving Institute. Institute name: Laxmi Driving Training. Website/domain branding: Laxmidriving.com. Target audience is Indian customers, including users who prefer very simple, clear wording. The school provides beginner and advanced driving training for cars, motorcycles and scooters.

Create these pages/routes and make navigation functional: Home, About Us, Car Training, Bike Training, Courses & Pricing, Instructors, Safety Rules, Testimonials, FAQ, Contact Us, and Book a Lesson.

Design: premium automotive style using Royal Blue #1E40E7 as primary, white #FFFFFF as background/contrast, and Orange #FF7A00 as accent. Use polished typography, strong hierarchy, rounded cards, subtle shadows, smooth but lightweight animations, excellent mobile/tablet/desktop responsiveness, and accessible contrast. Use attractive high-quality car and motorcycle imagery. Make the homepage conversion-focused with a hero headline, short trust-building copy, prominent “Book Your Lesson” CTA, secondary Call/WhatsApp CTAs, and clear Car/Bike training choices.

Include course cards for beginner and advanced car, motorcycle and scooter training, pricing placeholders that are easy to edit, instructor profiles, testimonials, safety rules, FAQ accordion, and a contact section with Google Maps embed/location placeholder. Add click-to-call and WhatsApp buttons. Booking form fields must be: Full Name, Mobile Number, E-mail ID, Vehicle Type (Car/Bike/Scooter), Experience Level (Beginner/Intermediate/Advanced), Preferred Date, Preferred Time, and Message. Include client-side validation and a clear success state; structure it so it can later connect to email/database without redesign.

Add SEO-friendly page titles, meta descriptions, semantic headings, descriptive image alt text, Open Graph basics, and clean URLs. Add a sticky mobile-friendly header and footer with navigation, contact details placeholders, and social links placeholders. Include a floating WhatsApp button and “Book Your Lesson” CTA throughout. Do not invent a real address, phone number, instructor names, prices, testimonials, or map coordinates; use clearly labeled editable placeholders where those details are unknown.

Use a polished Laxmi Driving Training brand identity consistently, with the wordmark “LAXMI DRIVING TRAINING” and website “LAXMIDRIVING.COM”. The final result should look like a real premium Indian driving-school website and be ready for preview/publishing.
